Already sliced it in Bambu Studio? Enter the filament weight it reports and the estimate uses that number instead, since that is what you will be charged for.

Rates per gram are from the Rapid Prototyping Centre site. PAHT-CF and multicolour are online orders only; the multicolour rate is the centre's stated average.

This is an unofficial estimate. Weight is modelled on Bambu Studio's 0.20 mm standard profile (0.42 mm lines, 5 top and 3 bottom layers, supports at 30° to a 15% grid). Supports are assumed to reach the build plate, so parts with internal overhangs may come in lighter. The slicer's weight on the day sets the real price.
